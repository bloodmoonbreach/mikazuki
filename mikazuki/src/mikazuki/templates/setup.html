AI help (optional)

An AI model can turn your existing CV into mikazuki's format and draft answers to application questions from your facts. Everything else works without it.

Model

You fill in your CV with a form, and Apply fills only the factual answers.

Free, private: nothing leaves your network. Needs a computer with a decent graphics card (a 12B model runs on 12 GB of video memory). Install Ollama, run ollama pull gemma4:12b, and let it listen on the network (OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0).

Any OpenAI-compatible API (OpenAI, Mistral, OpenRouter, Groq…). Usually costs a few cents a month at this volume.

Privacy: your CV, your notes and the job posts you draft answers for are sent to that service, and its terms decide what it keeps. Ollama keeps everything at home.
